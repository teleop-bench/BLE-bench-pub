--- Phase A resolution + U0/U1: THE FSU THROUGHPUT MEASUREMENT (2026-08-06) ---
Phase A diagnosis chain (fsu-throughput-plan.md): first-16-call trace showed
pools fill fast (55-193 us) -> completions paced at min 2392 us; payload-cap
test gave call_time = ~380 us + ~8 us/byte; PHY confirmed 2M during blast ->
ROOT CAUSE: the peripheral's default app-echo notified every blast write
back at full size - every byte flew TWICE; 94 KB/s each way = the 2M air
ceiling. 2392 us = 1048(TX) + 150 + 1048(echo) + 150 exactly. With sink mode
(CONFIG_APP_TPUT_SINK) completions dropped to min 1390 us = the one-way 2M
pair time (1048+150+44+150) - instrument now air-serialized by construction
(each write blocks until its pair completes on air).
Retractions now final: NOT the scheduler, NOT a host pump, NOT event
extension - the instrument's own echo.
